Hi Suma! 👋 This practice test covers Maryland alcohol & drug laws and safe-driving rules. After each answer you'll see right away if you got it right, and if not, why, plus a short rule to remember.

Tip: read the Key Concepts first, then take the test. Aim for 27/30 or better before your real exam.

📘 Key concepts (open to study first) Alcohol & BAC
  • 0.08 BAC or higher = Driving Under the Influence (DUI).
  • 0.07 BAC = evidence of Driving While Impaired (DWI). You can be charged below 0.08.
  • Under 21: no alcohol allowed. 0.02 or more violates the alcohol restriction.
  • 0.04 = limit when driving a commercial vehicle.
  • One standard drink = 12 oz beer (about 5%) = 5 oz wine = 1.5 oz 80-proof liquor.
  • The body removes about one standard drink per hour. Only time sobers you up. Coffee, cold showers, and food don't.
  • Refusing a breath/blood test: license suspended 270 days (1st), 2 years (2nd or more).
  • Test at 0.08 or higher: 180-day suspension (1st offense).
  • Open container of alcohol in the passenger area (seats and anywhere within reach) is illegal. Put it in the trunk.
  • Convicted of drunk/drugged driving with a child in the car: the judge can double the fine and jail time.
Drugs & cannabis
  • Cannabis is legal for adults 21+, but driving impaired by it is illegal, same as alcohol.
  • A driver may not smoke or consume cannabis in the passenger area (misdemeanor, 1 point).
  • Prescription and over-the-counter drugs: obey "do not drive" warning labels. You can be charged if they impair you.
Points system (any 2-year period)
  • 3–4 points: warning letter · 5–7: Driver Improvement Program · 8–11: suspension notice · 12+: revocation notice.
Phones & distraction
  • Adults: no handheld phone use while driving (only to start/end a call or turn the phone on/off). Primary offense, so police can stop you just for that. Texting is banned.
  • Under 18: no phone at all while driving, even hands-free, except to call 911.
Sharing the road
  • Move Over: for any stopped vehicle showing hazard lights, flares, cones, or emergency lights, move to a lane not next to it, or slow down if you can't.
  • School bus with flashing red lights: stop at least 20 feet away, in both directions, unless a physical median divides the road. Yellow flashing = slow down, get ready to stop.
  • Stop for pedestrians in marked or unmarked crosswalks. Never pass a car stopped at a crosswalk.
  • Pass bicycles by at least 3 feet.
  • Emergency vehicle with siren/lights: pull to the right edge, stop, and wait until it passes.
Belts & kids
  • Everyone in the car must wear a seat belt.
  • Children under 8 need a car seat or booster unless they are 4'9" or taller. Under 2 ride rear-facing (up to the seat's limits).
Safe driving
  • Following distance: at least 3–4 seconds in ideal conditions, more in bad weather or behind buses.
  • Hydroplaning: ease off the gas, don't brake, steer straight.
  • Drowsy: the only real fix is rest or switch drivers.
  • Aggressive drivers: don't compete or engage. Let them go.
  • Four-way stop, arriving together: yield to the driver on your right. Turning left: yield to oncoming traffic.
  • Speed limit = the max in ideal conditions. Slow down for rain, curves, traffic.
  • Work zones: flashing amber/yellow = workers present; flashing blue = active speed cameras. Slow down.
