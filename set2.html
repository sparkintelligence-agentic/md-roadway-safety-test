📘 Key concepts for Set 2 (open to study first) Points (counted over any 2 years)
  • 3–4 = warning letter · 5–7 = Driver Improvement Program · 8–11 = suspension · 12+ = revocation.
  • DWI conviction = 8 points (6-month suspension, 1 year if under 21). DUI conviction = 12 points.
  • Move Over: 1 point, or 3 if it contributes to a crash. Handheld phone: no points unless it contributes to a crash (then 3).
Testing & license consequences
  • Fail or refuse: the officer takes your license, and you may get a 45-day temporary paper license.
  • BAC 0.08–0.14: 180 days. BAC 0.15+: 180 days (1st), 270 days (2nd). Refusal: 270 days (1st), 2 years (2nd+).
  • Under 21: no alcohol at all. Tougher suspensions.
Ignition Interlock
  • A breath-test device in the car. The car won't start if it detects alcohol.
  • Time in program: 180 days (0.08–0.14), 1 year (0.15+ or refusal). Required after a DUI/DWI conviction or PBJ (since Oct 2024).
  • At 0.15+ or after a refusal, interlock is the only way to keep driving during the suspension.
Alcohol & drugs
  • Weight, body fat, food, tiredness, mood, and medicines all change how alcohol affects you. Counting drinks doesn't tell you you're safe.
  • A big mixed drink or strong craft beer can be 2–3 standard drinks.
  • Alcohol + medicines = stronger impairment. You can be charged even under 0.08.
  • No one may smoke cannabis in the passenger area. Open containers (even a six-pack with missing cans) go in the trunk.
Phones
  • No texting while driving, even stopped at a red light. Pull over and stop first.
Sharing the road
  • Move Over: change lanes away from it, or slow down if you can't.
  • School bus: a painted center turn lane is not a median, so stop. Wait until the lights are off and the bus is moving.
  • Siren behind you: pull right, clear of the intersection, stop.
  • Yield to: traffic already on the road (from a driveway/ramp), pedestrians when turning (green or right on red), cyclists in a bike lane you're crossing, and oncoming motorcycles when turning left.
  • Work zone lights: amber = workers, blue = speed cameras, green = maintenance vehicles.
Conditions & behavior
  • Wipers on = headlights on. Low beams in fog, rain, and snow.
  • Skid: release the pedal, steer where you want to go, and brake only once the tires grip again.
  • Following distance: 3–4 seconds minimum, 4–5+ behind buses and in bad weather.
  • Drowsy signs: yawning, drifting, missed exits, not remembering the last miles.
  • Rear-seat belts are secondary enforcement but still required for everyone.
After a crash
  • Injury: stay, call 911, report injuries and location, don't move the cars.
  • Hit an unattended car: find the owner or leave a note with your contact info.
  • Call police if someone is hurt, a car can't move, a driver seems impaired or unlicensed, a driver tries to leave without sharing info, or public property is damaged.
